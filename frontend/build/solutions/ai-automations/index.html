What We Automate

Six automation domains. One integrated system.

Lead Capture & Qualification

Stop losing leads to slow response times. AI agents that engage prospects the moment they arrive from qualifying intent, scoring leads, and routing the right ones to your sales team. Response times drop from hours to minutes.

Customer Support & Triage

Handle more queries without hiring more people. Intelligent support agents trained on your product, your tone, your FAQs. They resolve common queries instantly and escalate the complex ones with full context.

Outreach & Follow-up Sequences

Nurture at scale without losing the personal touch. Automated sequences that adapt to prospect behaviour, triggered by actions, timed to intent. Your pipeline stays warm even when your team is offline.

Internal Workflow Automation

Cut the admin, keep the output. From data entry and report generation to approvals and scheduling, we identify the manual bottlenecks in your operations and build agents that eliminate them.

Extract, transform, route automatically. AI pipelines that read documents, classify data, and trigger the right next step whether that’s updating a CRM, generating a summary, or notifying a team.

Reporting & Business Intelligence
