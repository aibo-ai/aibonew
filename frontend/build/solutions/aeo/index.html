Our approach

Position one isn’t the top anymore. Position zero is.

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) is the discipline of structuring content so search engines and AI assistants select it as the direct, authoritative answer to a user’s query displayed above organic results, read aloud by voice assistants, or embedded in AI-generated summaries.

Where SEO earns you a ranking, AEO earns you the answer box. That’s a fundamentally different form of value: the user gets their answer from you, without needing to click through. Your brand becomes the trusted source — not just another link in a list.

Our process

From first audit to cited default.

PHASE 01Weeks 1–2 (typical)
Query Intelligence Mapping

We identify every question your audience is actively asking using Google PAA data, Search Console queries, forum intelligence, and AI prompt analysis. We cluster these by intent and prioritise by snippet win-probability.

PHASE 02Weeks 2–3 (typical)
Competitor Snippet Audit

We audit which answer boxes your competitors currently own in your category and identify the structural weaknesses in their content that we can exploit to displace them.

DELIVERABLE
Competitor Snippet Analysis
PHASE 03Weeks 3–5 (typical)
Answer Architecture

We redesign your content structure to match what answer engines look for: concise 40–60 word definitions, step-structured how-to content, comparison tables, and FAQ schemas that align with specific query formats.

DELIVERABLE
Content Restructuring Plan + Implementation
PHASE 04Weeks 5–7 (typical)
Schema & Markup Implementation

We implement FAQPage, HowToStep, QAPage, and Speakable schema markup across your site signalling to Google and voice assistants exactly which content is answer-ready.

PHASE 05Ongoing
Track, Win, Defend

We monitor your snippet and PAA ownership weekly. When you win a box, we harden the content to defend it. When you lose one, we diagnose and recover. Position zero is dynamic — we treat it that way.
