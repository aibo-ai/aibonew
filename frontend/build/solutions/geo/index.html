The brand dominates searches for its own name — branded queries pull detailed, positive answers. But the moment a prompt drops the brand and just describes the job to be done, it disappears entirely, even on platforms that cite it elsewhere. That’s a citation-supply problem, not a brand-strength one — and it’s exactly what a MyAibo GEO audit is built to find and fix.

What we do

A complete GEO stack, run end-to-end.

Our approach

Search didn’t evolve. It reinvented itself.

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the practice of structuring your brand’s digital presence so AI language models surface you, not your competitors when users ask questions in your category.

Unlike traditional SEO, GEO isn’t about ranking on a page. It’s about becoming the trusted source that AI models draw from when they generate answers. That means owning how you’re represented across the web’s knowledge graph: your entity data, third-party citations, structured content, and E-E-A-T signals.

The Deliverables

What you walk away with.

01Brand Entity Optimisation

Wikidata profiles, Knowledge Panel management, Google Business structured data, and cross-web entity consistency so that AI so AI has one clean, authoritative version of your brand to draw from.

02Schema & Structured Data

Full implementation of JSON-LD markup across your website: Organisation, Product, FAQPage, HowTo, Article, BreadcrumbList structured for maximum AI readability.

03Third-Party Citation Building

Strategic placement across industry publications, forums, review platforms, and Q&A sites that AI engines crawl and trust. Every placement is contextually relevant, not spammy link-building.

04E-E-A-T Content Architecture

Content strategy built around Google’s Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust signals, the same signals LLMs use to evaluate sources. Includes author bios, expert attribution, and source citing.

05AI Citation Monitoring

Monthly audits across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google SGE. We track how often your brand appears, how accurately it’s described, and how your share-of-voice compares to competitors.

06Cross-Platform Presence Audit

Crunchbase, LinkedIn, G2, Glassdoor, industry wikis, and directory profiles, all aligned and optimised so AI models receive consistent, credible signals wherever they look.
