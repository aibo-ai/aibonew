The Shift

Search didn’t evolve. It reinvented itself.

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the practice of structuring your brand’s digital presence so AI language models surface you, not your competitors when users ask questions in your category.

Unlike traditional SEO, GEO isn’t about ranking on a page. It’s about becoming the trusted source that AI models draw from when they generate answers. That means owning how you’re represented across the web’s knowledge graph: your entity data, third-party citations, structured content, and E-E-A-T signals.

MyAibo is a GEO services provider based in India, working with brands who need GEO services in India built for how Indian and global AI platforms actually surface answers today — not a generic playbook borrowed from traditional SEO.

AI-generated answers now appear before organic results across every major platform. Brands that aren’t optimised for these surfaces are losing customers they never knew they had.

Our Process

Four phases to AI authority.

01

GEO Audit

We map your current brand entity across the web — Wikipedia, Wikidata, Google Knowledge Graph, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, and industry publications. We identify every place you’re missing, contradicted, or underrepresented in the data AI models train on.

Deliverable: GEO Readiness Report + Competitor Benchmarking
02

Architecture Build

We restructure your on-site content with schema markup (Organisation, FAQPage, HowTo, Article), optimise your structured data, and align your brand narrative to the citation patterns AI engines reward.

Deliverable: Schema Implementation + Content Blueprint
03

Authority Seeding

We place your brand in the third-party sources AI models trust most like industry publications, Q&A platforms, expert directories, and authoritative blogs. On platforms like Reddit, Quora, Wikipedia, and LinkedIn, we build a deliberate, brand-safe presence that AI engines actively cite.

Deliverable: Citation & Placement Campaign
04

Monitor & Compound

We run monthly AI citation audits across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and SGE tracking recommendation frequency, sentiment, and competitor share-of-voice. Then we iterate to compound your authority quarter after quarter.

Deliverable: Monthly GEO Performance Report + LLM Prompt Testing Report
