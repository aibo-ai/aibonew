Our approach

Content built for humans andAI engines equally.

Content marketing has always been about creating material that earns attention, builds trust, and drives action. What has changed is who and what you need to earn that attention from.

Today, great content must perform on three surfaces simultaneously: it needs to rank in Google (SEO), be selected as the authoritative answer by AI engines (GEO + AEO), and convert the human who ultimately reads it. Most content fails at least one of these. Often all three.

Random Content Creation

  • ✕AI engines ignore or misrepresent your content
  • ✕No amplification after publish

Our process

From first auditto cited default.

01Weeks 1–2 (typical)

Content Strategy & Audit

We audit your existing content library identifying what’s underperforming, what can be revived, and what gaps exist in your topic authority. We then build a content strategy that maps every planned piece to a business outcome: a keyword to win, a query to answer, an audience to convert.Content Strategy Document + Editorial Calendar
02Weeks 2–4 (typical)

Topic Authority Architecture

We design the full content architecture for your category: pillar pages that establish broad authority, cluster articles that support specific queries, FAQ hubs for AEO capture, and comparison content for high-intent buyers. Every piece connects to every other piece.Content Architecture Map
03Ongoing

Content Creation

Our senior writers produce content built to dual standards: rigorous enough for human readers, structured correctly for AI engines. Long-form guides, thought leadership, case studies, product pages, FAQs, video scripts, email sequences whatever format the strategy requires.Content Calendar Execution (ongoing)
04Ongoing

Optimisation & Structuring

Every piece is optimised before publishing: schema markup added, internal links woven in, answer blocks formatted for featured snippet capture, and E-E-A-T signals embedded like author attribution, expert citations, source linking.Published, Optimised Content
05Ongoing

Distribution & Seeding

We don’t publish and pray. Each piece is seeded across the platforms that matter: relevant Q&A forums, industry publications, social amplification, and newsletter distribution. Content that lives only on your site is underperforming its potential.Distribution Report per Piece
